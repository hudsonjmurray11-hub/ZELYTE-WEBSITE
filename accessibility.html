Our Commitment

Accessibility at ZELYTE

ZELYTE is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards across our website and digital properties.

We believe a clean, high-performance product should be matched by a clean, high-performance web experience — one that every person, regardless of ability, can use without friction.

Conformance Status

WCAG 2.1 Compliance

Partially Conformant — WCAG 2.1 Level AA

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. ZELYTE.shop is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard, and we are actively working to address those areas.

What We've Done

Measures Taken

We have taken the following steps to support accessibility across our site:

  • Semantic HTML5 elements used throughout (headings, nav, main, footer, section)
  • Descriptive alt text provided on all product and brand images
  • Color contrast ratios meet or exceed the WCAG 2.1 AA minimum of 4.5:1 for body text
  • All interactive elements (buttons, links, form fields) are keyboard-navigable
  • Focus indicators maintained for keyboard users
  • Forms include associated labels for screen reader compatibility
  • Font sizes use relative units (rem/em) to respect user browser preferences
  • No content flashes more than three times per second
Known Limitations

Areas We're Improving

Despite our efforts, some areas of our site have known accessibility limitations. We are working to resolve these:

  • Product image carousel — The swiper/carousel component on the product page has limited keyboard navigation support. We plan to improve arrow-key control and ARIA live region announcements.
  • Dynamic pricing updates — Animated price changes on the product page may not be announced to screen readers in all browsers. We are evaluating ARIA live region improvements.
  • Mobile menu — On small screens the navigation is hidden; a mobile-accessible menu is planned for a future update.

If you encounter a barrier not listed here, please let us know using the contact information below.

Formal Complaints

Escalation & Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility feedback, you may contact the relevant accessibility enforcement authority in your jurisdiction. In the United States, accessibility complaints related to digital services may be filed with the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division.

We are committed to working collaboratively and constructively to resolve all accessibility concerns.
