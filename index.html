Our Story

I came up with Zelyte my senior year of high school, after years of rowing, wrestling, and lifting. I knew the feeling of being 1,000 meters into a race with a dry mouth and cramping legs, no way to stop, no way to drink, just suffering through it. I watched my teammates reach for nicotine pouches and thought: Why can't that format actually do something useful?

I spent the next summer in my kitchen mixing prototypes and tracking down manufacturers. When I got to college, I built our team, pitched the idea, won, and haven't stopped working since.

Zelyte launches this summer.

Hudson Murray

Founder & CEO

01

Place

Tuck a ZELYTE pouch between your upper lip and gum. It sits comfortably and discreetly — no hands needed.

02

The pouch slowly releases electrolytes and caffeine, absorbed directly through your gums via buccal absorption (absorbed through your gum lining).

03

Perform

Stay hydrated and focused for 20–40 minutes. No drinking. No stopping. No compromise.
